Half the pointswithin about 3 points of accuracy

How many points does a 3D classifier need?

A controlled benchmark of 3 samplers, 4 model variants and 2 datasets over 3 seeds. On real scans (ScanObjectNN), 512 points kept accuracy within 2.8 points and made DGCNN 58% faster to train. On CAD models (ModelNet40), 256 points was enough. Rare classes suffer first when density drops.
